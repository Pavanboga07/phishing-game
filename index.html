Basics

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers pose as legitimate organizations to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or social security details.

💡 90% of all data breaches start with a phishing email.
Types

The Phishing Spectrum

  • Spear Phishing: Targeted attacks against specific individuals or companies.
  • Smishing: Phishing via SMS or text messages.
  • Vishing: Voice phishing using phone calls or AI clones.
  • Whaling: Targeting high-level executives (CEOs, CFOs).
Detection

Major Red Flags

  • 🚩 Artificial Urgency: Threatening account closure or legal action.
  • 🚩 Suspicious Domains: Lookalike URLs (e.g., paypa1.com).
  • 🚩 Unexpected Attachments: Invoices or documents you didn't ask for.
  • 🚩 Generic Greeting: Addressing you as "Dear Member" instead of your name.
Prevention

How to Protect Yourself

  • ✅ Enable MFA: Always use Multi-Factor Authentication.
  • ✅ Verify the Source: Contact the company through their official website.
  • ✅ Hover Before You Click: Check where a link actually leads.
  • ✅ Use a Password Manager: Helps prevent entering credentials into fake sites.
